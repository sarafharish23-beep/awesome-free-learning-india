# Awesome Free Learning (India)

An honest, curated list of free learning resources that are useful to learners in India. "Free" here means you can learn without paying. Some platforms charge for an exam or a certificate, and we say so where we know.

Contributions are welcome. Please add only resources that are genuinely free to learn from, and say clearly when a certificate or exam costs money.

## Government platforms
- [SWAYAM](https://swayam.gov.in) – Government of India MOOCs, from school to postgraduate level. Courses are free; the proctored certificate exam usually has a fee.
- [NPTEL](https://nptel.ac.in) – engineering and science courses from IITs and IISc. Free videos; the certification exam is paid.
- [DIKSHA](https://diksha.gov.in) – NCERT/CBSE and state-board school content for students and teachers.
- [NIOS](https://www.nios.ac.in) – the National Institute of Open Schooling, with open schooling and free study material.
- [e-Pathshala](https://epathshala.nic.in) – NCERT textbooks and e-resources.
- [NCERT textbooks](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php) – free PDFs of all NCERT books.
- [Skill India Digital Hub](https://www.skillindiadigital.gov.in) – government skilling courses.

## Coding and tech
- [freeCodeCamp](https://www.freecodecamp.org) – a full web development and Python curriculum with free certifications.
- [The Odin Project](https://www.theodinproject.com) – a free full-stack web curriculum.
- [CS50 (Harvard)](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/) – an introduction to computer science; free to take.
- [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/training/) – free modules for Azure, Power BI, Excel and more. Certification exams are paid.

## School, maths and science
- [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org) – maths and science practice. It is also available in Hindi at [hi.khanacademy.org](https://hi.khanacademy.org).
- [PhET Simulations](https://phet.colorado.edu) – free interactive science and maths simulations.

## Languages
- [BBC Learning English](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish) – free English lessons and audio.
- [Duolingo](https://www.duolingo.com) – free language practice app.

## Skills and exams in Hindi and English
- [YUNOVA](https://yunova.in) – free, level-by-level paths built from the best YouTube teachers, for skills like [Excel](https://yunova.in/skills/excel), [Typing](https://yunova.in/skills/typing-data-entry), [Tally](https://yunova.in/skills/tally) and [AutoCAD](https://yunova.in/skills/autocad), and syllabus-ordered study paths for exams like [CTET](https://yunova.in/study/ctet) and [REET](https://yunova.in/study/reet). Skills have topic tests, a final test and a verifiable certificate. Supported by ads. *(Disclosure: this list is maintained by the YUNOVA team.)*

## Tips
- Pick one resource per subject and finish it before collecting more.
- Practise every day: one topic plus a small exercise is better than a long weekend binge.
- Check certificate terms before you start if a certificate matters to you.
